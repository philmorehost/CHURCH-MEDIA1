# 🎙️ Church Media System & Mobile App — Presentation Script & Speaker Notes

**Target Audience:** Church Leadership, Pastors, Department Heads, and Congregation Members
**Meeting Date:** Upcoming Sunday Service & Workers' Meeting

## 📌 Slide 1: Title Slide — Welcome & Introduction

### 🗣️ Speaker Notes:
> *"Praise the Lord, everyone! Today, I am excited to introduce our official Church Media Management System and Mobile App. This digital platform has been specifically designed to unify our website, mobile application, and parish network so that everyone in our congregation—whether in the sanctuary, at home, or anywhere across the world—can stay connected to the life and ministry of our church."*

### 🔑 Key Points to Highlight:
* Available on both Web Browser and Google Play Store for Android.
* Built-in Full-Screen Media Feed (Reels), Offline Holy Bible, Audio Player, Live Broadcasts, and Online Giving.

---

## 📌 Slide 2: Vision & Executive Summary — A Digital Ministry Without Walls

### 🗣️ Speaker Notes:
> *"Our vision is simple: to create a digital ministry without walls. In today's fast-paced world, people consume media on their phones daily. By launching our own vertical reel feed, audio Bible reader, and instant push notification system, we are bringing the Word of God, worship moments, and church updates straight into people's hands every day."*

### 🔑 Key Points to Highlight:
* **Digital Outreach:** Connecting youth, families, and remote members.
* **Unified Network:** Province → Zone → Area → Parish structure linking every branch church.

---

## 📌 Slide 3: Core Experience — Full-Screen Media Reels & Feed

### 🗣️ Speaker Notes:
> *"When you open the website or mobile app, the first thing you see is our interactive Media Feed. It features full-screen 9:16 vertical reels—just like Instagram or TikTok. You can watch sermon clips, worship moments, youth nights, and testimony videos. Members can like posts, save them for later, and comment with threaded replies or images."*

### 🔑 Key Points to Highlight:
* **Categories:** Filter by Worship, Sermon Clip, Youth, Testimony, or Events.
* **Pinned Reels:** Leadership can pin up to 3 important reels at the top for 3 days to ensure top announcements are seen first.

---

## 📌 Slide 4: Spiritual Growth — Holy Bible & Voice-Assisted Audio Reader

### 🗣️ Speaker Notes:
> *"For daily spiritual growth, we have embedded a full Holy Bible inside the platform. What makes this special is our Voice Reading feature. You can tap Play, and the system will read scripture aloud to you, highlighting each verse as it speaks and auto-scrolling down the chapter. You can even adjust the speed from 0.75x to 1.5x speed. Even better—the KJV and BBE versions work 100% offline without any internet connection!"*

### 🔑 Key Points to Highlight:
* **Audio Reader:** Natural voice synthesis with real-time active verse highlighting.
* **Offline Access:** KJV and BBE read offline; online versions include NIV, NLT, and NKJV with instant verse search.

---

## 📌 Slide 5: Global Leadership & Broadcasts — G.O. Declaration & Live Services

### 🗣️ Speaker Notes:
> *"At the very top of our website, we feature the General Overseer's Prophetic Declaration. Whether set as a scrolling marquee banner or a fixed top banner, members can immediately see the G.O.'s theme and prophetic word for the year. Additionally, whenever our service is live, a 'LIVE' badge turns on, allowing anyone to tune in to our live broadcast with a single tap."*

### 🔑 Key Points to Highlight:
* **G.O. Declaration Banner:** Prominent top banner with prophetic word and yearly theme.
* **Live Broadcasts & Sermon Archive:** Direct streaming and searchable sermon library with scripture references and audio MP3 downloads.

## 📌 Slide 6: Generosity & Community — Online Giving & Parish Directory

### 🗣️ Speaker Notes:
> *"Supporting the ministry is easier than ever. Members can give Tithes, Offerings, Building Fund gifts, and Special Seed pledges through secure online card checkout via Payhub, or choose manual bank transfer and upload their transfer receipt. On the administration side, our finance team has a dedicated dashboard to verify bank transfer receipts and export financial audit reports."*

### 🔑 Key Points to Highlight:

* **Parish Directory:** Dedicated parish pages, service times, and leadership contacts.

## 📌 Slide 7: Monetization & Outreach — 9:16 Vertical Advertising System

### 🗣️ Speaker Notes:
> *"For business owners, entrepreneurs, and partner ministries in our church, we have launched an Advertising Placement System. You can submit vertical 9:16 video or image ads to display across our website and mobile app. Once approved by the admin team, your ad goes live immediately! Advertisers receive a secure Publisher Portal link sent to their email to track live impressions, clicks, CTR, and receive daily email reports."*

### 🔑 Key Points to Highlight:
* **Advert Placement (`/advertise`):** Submit 9:16 vertical ads with custom duration and display frequency.
* **Publisher Portal (`/ad-manager`):** Live analytics and daily automated performance summary emails.
